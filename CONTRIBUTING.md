# Contributing to The Five Laws of AI Agents

Contributions and rigorous criticism are welcome. This is independent, non-peer-reviewed research; acceptance of a contribution does not imply scientific validation or endorsement.

## How to contribute

1. Open an issue identifying the manuscript **chapter/section**, case ID, evidence entry, or file and line. Explain the problem, cite sources, and distinguish a normative disagreement from a factual error or software bug.
2. For code changes, include a minimal reproduction, expected and observed behavior, and tests. Run `python -m unittest test_reference_gate test_analysis_template test_additional_integrity -v`, `python validate_corpus.py`, and `python verify_release.py` on a clean release before submitting.
3. For research cases, describe the hypothesis, assumptions, adjudication uncertainties, and why the new case challenges the framework. Do not present synthetic labels as independently adjudicated ground truth.
4. For textual changes, retain the author's original thesis faithfully, note the edition, and supply precise references. Do not silently rewrite the historical manuscript PDF; propose a new version for substantive changes.
5. Do not post private information, confidential prompts, security credentials, copyrighted full texts, or personal case records.

## Licensing of contributions

By contributing, you confirm you have the necessary rights and agree that contributions to original research text, data, and documentation are made under **CC BY 4.0**, and contributions to software files under **Apache License 2.0**, as specified in [`RIGHTS_AND_ATTRIBUTION.md`](RIGHTS_AND_ATTRIBUTION.md). Do not submit incompatible third-party material. Video contributions require separate rights review.

## Scientific status

The educational reference gate is not a deployable security boundary. Empirical outcome files are blank templates until independent studies are conducted. Respect the difference between hypotheses, self-tests, and independent evidence.
