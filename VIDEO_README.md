## Video overview

This **AI-generated video and soundtrack** offer an accessible, illustrative introduction to the Five Laws, delegated authority, corrigibility, and the Delegation–Entitlement Firewall. The [GitHub README](README.md#video-explainer) displays a local sharp-resolution preview image that opens the video on **YouTube**. The video is hosted there rather than copied into the GitHub source repository, keeping clone and upload sizes small and consolidating public video viewing on the original YouTube page.

The video is **educational communication, not experimental evidence**, peer review, an independent validation, or proof of production AI-agent safety. The original 69-page research manuscript remains the authoritative scholarly work. The Zenodo v1.0.0 archive deliberately contains **no** video or audio.
